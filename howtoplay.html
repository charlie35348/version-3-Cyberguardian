How to Play

  • Enter your username on the Threats page before beginning any challenge.
  • Select a cyber threat category — Password, Privacy, Phishing, or Cyberbullying — to start your challenge.
  • Read each scenario carefully. It could be a genuine signal or a crafted scam.
  • Choose the answer you believe is correct from the four options provided.
  • A correct answer awards +50 points. An incorrect answer deducts −50 points.
  • Each threat level contains 5 scenarios, for a maximum of 250 points per level.
  • Use the Previous and Next buttons to navigate between answered questions.
  • You must score at least 200 points to pass a level. Your result is shown at the end.
  • All scores are saved to the scoreboard — compete against others and improve each run.
  • Think smart: cyber safety depends on the decisions you make, not just what you know.
